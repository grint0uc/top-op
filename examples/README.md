Example meshes, generated by `uv run python examples/make_examples.py` (committed; units are mm).
`cantilever.stl`: 60 x 20 x 20 box with its min corner at the origin; the classic cantilever beam.
`bracket.stl`: L-bracket, 80 x 60 x 10 base plate plus a 10 x 60 wall along x=0 rising to z=60.
The base plate has four 6 mm through-holes (x = 20 and 72, y = 8 and 52), clear of the wall.
The wall has one 12 mm through-hole along X, centered at y=30, z=35; bounding box is 80 x 60 x 60.
Both meshes are watertight (asserted by the generator and by `tests/test_contract.py`).
