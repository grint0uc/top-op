      About top-op lite

      A lightweight, in-browser version of top-op, the open-source 3D topology optimization tool. Everything here runs on your device: the part is voxelized, then a SIMP optimizer removes material that carries little load until the target volume fraction is reached.

      • Solver: 8-node hexahedra (E = 1, ν = 0.3), matrix-free Jacobi-preconditioned CG in a Web Worker, density filter, optimality-criteria update.
      • Limits: coarse grids (about 40 000 elements), one load case, STL input only (no STEP), no stress constraint, no symmetry or overhang control. The full app has all of these.
      • Units: none enforced. Use a consistent set such as mm, N and MPa. Because E = 1, compliance (N·mm) scales as 1/E; divide by your modulus in MPa for physical values.
      • Supports fix x, y and z on the grid nodes within 0.75 h of the selected faces; a load's force is the total, split evenly over its nodes.
